Feed-first real dashboard

This keeps the current light control-room styling, but changes the phone order so the trade feed appears in the first viewport. Account, risk, positions, and follows become compact summary surfaces instead of blocking the audit stream.

  • Top area stays under 170px: title, worker status, poll freshness, and active follows.
  • KPIs become a horizontal rail, so they are available but no longer push the trade feed down.
  • Filters stay close to the feed and can become sticky while scrolling.
  • Risk rules, positions, and follows move below the first few orders as collapsed rows.
